Masala Mingle is a ticketed South Asian cultural experience combining curated vendors, authentic food, structured community conversations, and an evening social mixer. Two experiences in one day, built for young SA professionals during AAPI Heritage Month.

The Experience

Six pillars.
One immersive cultural day.

01
Marketplace

50 application-vetted, South Asian-founded brands across beauty, fashion, art, jewelry, home goods, and tech.

02
Food Stalls

5–6 in-house food stalls serving authentic South Asian street food in a fully equipped commercial kitchen.

03
The Mingle

Structured 20-min rotating conversation rounds on diaspora, identity & culture. Where strangers become community.

04
Content Corner

Branded photo backdrop & installation. Built for social media moments and cultural storytelling.

05
Drinks + Lounge

A place to breathe and connect — whether you're sipping wine, chai, or a signature cocktail.

06
Jam Session

Evening live music, cocktails, mingling, and nightlife energy — SA culture at its most electric.
